## Finance amount and Oxford fee rules

- Principal loan amount is the amount requested by the applicant.
- Funder documentation fee: 1% of principal.
- VAT: 15% of principal plus the 1% documentation fee.
- Total finance amount: `(principal + documentation fee) × 1.15`.
- Oxford's facilitation and administration fees are separate, partner-specific commercial terms; do not add VAT again to Oxford fees.
- No universal default partner fee rates are permitted. An approved active terms version for that specific partner is required.

## Acceptance evidence states

Use these exact distinctions: implemented in source; automated tests passing; Deploy Preview verified; real provider/account test verified; awaiting business approval; blocked by missing configuration/external action; not yet implemented.

## Release restrictions

No production deployment, live applications, document upload, email/WhatsApp delivery, live settlement execution, fund transfer or payout processing until separate approval and acceptance evidence are recorded. Preview-only settlement recording requires active partner-specific terms and non-production data. Legal and regulatory readiness requires qualified professional review.
